Apply for your Canadian Armed Forces pension

Use this service if you are releasing, or have released, from the Regular Force or Reserve Force and want to start your pension benefits. It takes about 25 minutes, and you can save and come back at any time.

Before you start, have these ready

  • Your service number and Social Insurance Number
  • Your Pension Benefit Options Statement, if you have received it
  • Your spouse or common-law partner's name and date of birth
  • Your banking information: institution number, transit (branch) number and account number. A void cheque or a direct deposit form from your bank shows all three.

Sign in to start

Choose how you want to sign in. Your progress is saved to your account.

Sign in with GCKey Sign in with your bank (Sign-In Partner) Still serving? Sign in through CWA

Protected B when completed. Your personal information is collected under the Canadian Forces Superannuation Act and used only to administer your pension.
